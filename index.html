daybook.

Privacy Policy — last updated September 23, 2026

daybook. is a home screen launcher for Android. This policy explains what the app accesses on your device and why.

What daybook. accesses

To function as a home screen launcher, daybook. requires the following:

  • Default launcher (home app) role. daybook. registers as a home screen replacement so it can be set as your device's launcher. This is required for any launcher app to work at all.
  • List of installed apps. daybook. reads the list of apps installed on your device in order to display them in the app drawer and let you choose apps to pin. This information is used only to build the on-device app list and is never transmitted anywhere.

What daybook. does not do

  • daybook. does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server.
  • daybook. does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • daybook. does not access your contacts, messages, photos, location, or any data belonging to other apps beyond their name and icon for display purposes.
  • daybook. has no network permissions beyond what Android grants by default; the app does not make network requests.

Data storage

Any preferences you set (such as pinned apps, layout choices, or display options) are stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling daybook. removes this data.

Changes to this policy

daybook. is under active development. As features are added, this policy will be updated to accurately reflect what the app accesses and why. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed via the contact information listed on daybook.'s Play Store listing.
